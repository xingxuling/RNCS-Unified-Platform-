# Native Visual Uplift Report

Status: pending-human-review

Backend: rncs-native-2d25d-cpu

The automated report records a structural comparison against the Phase 4 baseline. Human visual acceptance and commercial Anime quality remain separate gates.

- character_structure: explicit torso, neck, head, shoulder, arm, hand and rig nodes
- face: 3/4 head, asymmetric eye spacing, brows, nose and jaw controls
- hair_costume: hair groups and costume panels with lagged chains and occlusion order
- space_contact: perspective camera, depth layers, contact receiver and active occluder
- lighting_line_color: compiled contour/cel/rim/material laws and light layer
- performance: gaze-first head/shoulder/weight turn with anticipation, overshoot and settle
- temporal_continuity: body/performance/scene/style/frame roots plus local repair scope
