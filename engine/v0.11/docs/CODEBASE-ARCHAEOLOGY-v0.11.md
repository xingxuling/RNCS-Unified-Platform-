# Codebase Archaeology v0.11

v0.10 already manufactured accepted assets and instantiated RSR/VSR entities. The remaining shared bottleneck for software, games and video was not another asset or rendering facade: it was executable intent over time. The minimum change surface was therefore Reality Behavior quests and deterministic replay, plus a Reality Studio sequencer whose authority events and presentation tracks are sealed separately. Network remains v0.2 because this release does not introduce a second physical authority protocol.
