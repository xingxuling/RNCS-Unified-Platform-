# Codebase Archaeology v0.10

RAGF v0.3 already generated GLB, PBR textures, rigs, animations, LOD, collision, Prefab, lineage and continuity bundles. Reality Studio v1.4 already imported assets and edited RSR/VSR spatial worlds. The minimum change surface was therefore a persistent production session, candidate review and targeted regeneration in RAGF, plus a real acceptance workflow in Studio that instantiates a scene node and RSR embodiment. Network, RSR and VSR protocols were deliberately preserved because this release changes manufacturing rather than authority transport.
