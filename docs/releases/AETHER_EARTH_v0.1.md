# Aether Earth v0.1 — RCL Collective Intelligence Earth Sandbox

## Product closure

Aether Earth is the first integrated product that exercises RNCS/RCL as a world system rather than as isolated infrastructure. It combines world state, life, knowledge, accelerated time, reversible compression, scientific evidence and generated executable structures.

## Runtime structure

```text
Earth tiles → 100 organisms → observations → RCLpedia revisions
     ↓                ↓                ↓
accelerated time   social exchange   scientific aggregation
     ↓                ↓                ↓
compressed state ← collective crystal → isolated RCL native verification
```

## Current scale

- 256 Earth tiles across four climate families;
- 100 persistent organisms and four genome archetypes;
- 512 generated seed knowledge claims plus runtime-derived claims;
- day simulation, event leap and 1×/10×/100× logical clocks;
- lossless deflate world capsules with SHA-256 reality roots;
- generated RCL crystal source, RBC bytecode and native VM execution evidence;
- browser cockpit and Android source host with foreground service/JobScheduler.

## Non-claims

This is not a high-fidelity physical Earth model, a foundation model, or proof of emergent general intelligence. Android background execution remains subject to platform scheduling and energy policies. The Android APK was not built in this container because no Android SDK/Gradle toolchain was present.
