## Acceptance Gates

### Gate A: Anatomy Validation Pack

- Ten named views are generated from one genome, one morphogenesis runtime and one renderer.
- Every view contains visible character anatomy, not a debug card or a repeated frame.
- Front, 3/4 left, 3/4 right and side preserve identity anchors.
- Head, shoulder, elbow and neutral/action pose checks pass without human acceptance being auto-promoted.

### Gate B: Rebuilt Native Shot

- Five seconds, 120 frames, 1280x720, 24fps, real WAV and real MP4.
- Same character and scene semantics as the Phase 6 audit baseline.
- Body surface, face, hair, joint and hand roots are in the render plan and ledger.
- A/B comparison includes the named dimensions from the brief.

### Gate C: Surgery and Rollback

- One residual is detected, mapped to a root cause and repaired locally.
- Episode Intent Root and Character Identity Root remain unchanged.
- Unaffected frames are preserved and the base version remains restorable.

## Risks

- A deterministic procedural surface can pass structural checks while remaining visually weak.
- 2.5D projection may not prove full 3D anatomy or production-grade deformation.
- Human visual acceptance may reject the uplift even when all automated checks pass.
- The unmerged Phase 6 PR may later change shared interfaces; this branch must document any rebase or integration work explicitly.

## Rollback

- Roll back the local surgery candidate to the base body surface and preserve the receipt.
- Keep Episode, Character Identity and Genome roots immutable.
- Rebuild only the affected validation views or frame interval.
- Do not delete the failed evidence; retain it as a rejected candidate with its failure reason.

## Unprovable Claims

- This review does not prove commercial Anime quality, professional character design, full 3D parity, Godot/Unity parity or human visual acceptance.
- A passing machine check does not authorize `human_visual_acceptance=accepted`.
- A rebuilt MP4 alone does not prove that body anatomy is correct; the Anatomy Validation Pack is the first authority for this phase.
